% Feature specification. Kept in sync with the code by source/tests/test_feature_appendix.py:
% every descriptor name returned by the extractors must appear below as \f{name}.
\providecommand{\f}[1]{\texttt{\detokenize{#1}}}

Let a track be a sequence of centroids $p_1,\dots,p_n$ with frame indices, $\Delta p_i=p_{i+1}-p_i$,
$v_i=\lVert\Delta p_i\rVert$ the speed in pixels per frame, $\phi=\mathrm{fps}/s$ the effective frame rate
($s$ the frame-sampling step, 1 in all reported runs), $T=n/\phi$ the track duration in seconds, and
$\theta_i=\operatorname{atan2}(\Delta p_{i,y},\Delta p_{i,x})$ the heading. Speeds in px/s are $v_i\phi$.
Per-track descriptors are pooled over a video's tracks by mean, maximum and standard deviation (population
$\sigma$; zero for one track); a descriptor that is undefined for a track (too few points, or below the minimum
length) is simply absent for that track.

\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Shape (6), $n\ge 4$}} \\

\f{convex_hull_area}, \f{compactness} & area of the centroids' convex hull; $4\pi\,\text{area}/\text{perimeter}^2$ \\

\f{mean_relative_speed_px_s}, \f{max_relative_speed_px_s} & $\lvert v^{A}_i-v^{B}_i\rvert\,\phi$ over shared frames \\
\f{proximity_duration_ratio} & share of shared frames at distance $<100$ px \\
\f{proximity_event_rate_s} & number of entries into the 100 px neighbourhood per second of overlap \\
